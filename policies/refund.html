Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Before you order

Please check your order carefully before paying — quantities and delivery address. Because these are consumable alcohol products, we're more limited in what we can accept back than a typical retailer.

2. Order cancellation before dispatch

If you need to cancel, contact us as soon as possible after paying. If your order hasn't been dispatched yet, we can cancel it and refund you in full. Once it's out for delivery, see Section 3 instead.

3. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, is faulty, or doesn't match what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo of the item and your order number. We'll arrange a replacement or refund for the affected item once we've confirmed the issue. Nothing in this policy affects your rights under applicable Singapore consumer protection laws.

4. What we can't accept back

For food-safety and regulatory reasons, we can't accept returns of alcohol that has been opened, or returns based on a simple change of mind, once an order has been delivered.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued back to the card or payment method you used to pay, via Stripe. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement after we issue it. We don't refund to a different payment method or as store credit.

Delivery fees are refunded only if the entire order is cancelled or the issue was our error (e.g. we sent the wrong item or it arrived damaged in transit).

6. Order errors on our side

If we're out of stock on something after you've paid, we'll contact you and refund that item — you won't be charged for anything you don't receive.

7. How to request a refund

Contact us on WhatsApp +65 9868 0555 with your order number and a description of the issue (and a photo, if it's about a damaged or wrong item). We'll confirm what happens next by reply.

1. 下单前请仔细核对

请在付款前仔细核对您的订单——包括数量和收货地址。由于所售商品为消耗性酒类产品，我们能够接受的退货范围比一般商品零售商更为有限。

2. 订单发货前取消

如需取消订单，请在付款后尽快与我们联系。如果订单尚未发货，我们可以为您全额取消并退款。一旦订单已在配送途中，请参见第3条。

3. 商品损坏、有缺陷或错发

如果商品到货时损坏、存在缺陷或与您所订购的不符，请尽快与我们联系，建议在收货后48小时内联系，并提供商品照片及订单号。我们在确认问题后，会为受影响的商品安排更换或退款。本政策不影响您依据新加坡适用消费者保护法律所享有的权利。

4. 不予接受退货的情形

出于食品安全及法规要求，一旦订单已送达，我们无法接受已开封酒类的退货，也无法接受仅因客户改变主意而提出的退货。

5. 退款如何处理

经确认的退款会通过 Stripe 退回至您付款所使用的银行卡或支付方式。根据发卡银行不同，退款到账通常需要5–10个工作日。我们不会将退款转到其他支付方式，也不提供购物金形式的退款。

配送费仅在整单取消，或问题由我们造成（例如发错商品或运输途中损坏）时才会退还。

6. 我方原因导致的订单问题

如果您付款后我们发现商品缺货，我们会与您联系并为该商品办理退款——您不会为未收到的商品付费。

7. 如何申请退款

请通过 WhatsApp 联系我们：+65 9868 0555，并提供订单号及问题说明（如涉及商品损坏或错发，请附上照片）。我们会通过回复确认后续处理方式。
