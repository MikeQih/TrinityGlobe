Privacy Policy

Internal draft — prepared for review, not yet published to customers.

This is a first-pass draft, not final legal text. It describes what the site actually collects and where it actually goes, based on how checkout is built (see the PRD and supabase/migrations/ in the repo). It has not been checked against Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) by a qualified reviewer — do that before publishing. Anything marked like this still needs a decision or confirmation.

1. What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Delivery address and postal code (not collected if you choose self-collection)
  • Order details — items, quantities, amount paid
  • Any notes you add to your order

We don't collect or store your payment card details — those go directly to our payment processor, Stripe (see Section 3).

2. How we use it

  • To process and fulfil your order, including contacting you about delivery
  • To send an order confirmation and, if something goes wrong, to reach you about it
  • To verify you meet the legal age requirement for purchasing alcohol
  • To meet our own record-keeping and tax obligations

We don't sell your information. We don't send marketing email unless you separately opt in to that — confirm whether marketing email is planned; if not, remove this line.

3. Payment information

Payment is handled by Stripe. When you pay, your card details go directly to Stripe over an encrypted connection — they never pass through our own servers or database. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant. Their handling of your data is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.

4. Where your data is stored

Order and account data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, behind access controls that restrict it to staff whose role requires it (see our internal Row Level Security policies). Order confirmation and internal notification emails are sent through Resend.

5. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Stripe — to process your payment
  • Supabase — to store your order
  • Resend — to send order emails
  • Our delivery courier/partner [name once confirmed] — your name, phone number, and delivery address, so they can deliver your order

We disclose personal data beyond this only if required by Singapore law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes (confirm retention period, e.g. 5 years per IRAS record-keeping requirements). You can ask us to delete personal data we're not legally required to retain — see Section 7.

7. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop using or delete it (subject to our legal retention obligations). To do any of this, contact us using the details in Section 9.

8. Cookies

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember what's in your cart between visits. This stays on your device — it isn't sent to us until you check out. Confirm whether any analytics/marketing cookies are in use; if so, add them here.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or to exercise your rights above: WhatsApp +65 9868 0555 or email orders@trinityglobe.sg [confirm this mailbox is live].
