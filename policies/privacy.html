Privacy Policy

Internal draft — prepared for review, not yet published to customers.

1. What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Delivery address and postal code (not collected if you choose self-collection)
  • Order details — items, quantities, amount paid
  • Any notes you add to your order

If you create an account instead of checking out as a guest, we also collect:

  • Your name and email address from Google or Facebook, if you sign in that way
  • Date of birth, gender, and newsletter preference, if you sign up with email and password (date of birth doubles as an age check for an alcohol business, not just a profile field)
  • Any delivery addresses you choose to save to your account

We don't collect or store your payment card details — those go directly to our payment processor, Stripe (see Section 3).

2. How we use it

  • To process and fulfil your order, including contacting you about delivery
  • To send an order confirmation and, if something goes wrong, to reach you about it
  • To verify you meet the legal age requirement for purchasing alcohol
  • To let you sign in and see your own order history and saved addresses
  • To meet our own record-keeping and tax obligations

We don't sell your information. We don't send marketing email unless you separately tick the newsletter opt-in when creating an account.

3. Payment information

Payment is handled by Stripe. When you pay, your card details go directly to Stripe over an encrypted connection — they never pass through our own servers or database. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant. Their handling of your data is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.

4. Where your data is stored

Order and account data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, behind access controls (Row Level Security) that restrict it to you and to staff whose role requires it. Order confirmation and internal notification emails are sent through Resend. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, that sign-in itself is handled by Supabase's authentication service — we receive your name and email from them, not your Google/Facebook password.

5. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Stripe — to process your payment
  • Supabase — to store your order and account data, and to handle Google/Facebook sign-in
  • Resend — to send order emails
  • Google / Facebook — only if you choose to sign in that way, to confirm your identity
  • Our delivery courier — your name, phone number, and delivery address, so they can deliver your order

We disclose personal data beyond this only if required by Singapore law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for at least 5 years, in line with IRAS record-keeping requirements for tax purposes. You can ask us to delete personal data we're not legally required to retain — see Section 7.

7. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop using or delete it (subject to our legal retention obligations). To do any of this, contact us using the details in Section 9.

8. Cookies

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember what's in your cart, and — if you're signed in — to keep you signed in between visits. This stays on your device and isn't sent to us until you check out or make a request that needs it. We don't currently use analytics or marketing cookies/trackers.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or to exercise your rights above: WhatsApp +65 9868 0555 or email orders@trinityglobe.sg.
