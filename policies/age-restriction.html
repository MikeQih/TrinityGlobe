1. You must be 18 or older

The legal age to purchase or consume alcohol in Singapore is 18. By placing an order on this site, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. It is an offence under Singapore law to supply alcohol to someone under 18, and to purchase alcohol on behalf of someone under 18.

2. Confirmation at checkout

You'll be asked to confirm your age before you can pay. We're not able to process an order without this confirmation.

3. Verification at delivery or collection

Whoever receives the order — at your door, or at our self-collection point — must be 18 or older and may be asked to show a valid photo ID (NRIC, FIN card, or passport) before we hand the order over.
