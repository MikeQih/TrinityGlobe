Responsible Drinking & Age Restriction Notice

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. You must be at least 18 years old

You must be at least 18 years old to purchase or receive alcohol from us. By placing an order on this site, you confirm that you meet this requirement. It is an offence under Singapore law to supply alcohol to someone under 18, and to purchase alcohol on behalf of someone under 18.

2. Confirmation at checkout

You'll be asked to confirm your age before you can pay. We're not able to process an order without this confirmation.

3. Verification at delivery

Whoever receives the order at your door must be 18 or older and may be asked to show a valid photo ID (NRIC, FIN card, or passport) before we hand the order over. If ID can't be produced, or the person receiving it appears to be under 18, we will not hand over the order — see our Delivery Policy for what happens next.

4. We don't market to minors

Our marketing is directed at adults of legal drinking age. We don't knowingly target or advertise to anyone under 18.

5. Drink responsibly

We encourage responsible, moderate consumption. If you or someone you know would like support relating to alcohol use, visit the National Addictions Management Service.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: WhatsApp +65 9868 0555.
